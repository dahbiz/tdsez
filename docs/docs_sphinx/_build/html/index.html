TDSE-Z: Time-Dependent Schrödinger Equation Solver

TDSE-Z solves the time-dependent Schrödinger equation (TDSE) for a single particle in one, two and three dimensions, from atoms and molecules in strong laser fields to semiconductor heterostructures with a position-dependent effective mass. Space is discretised with B-splines through isogeometric analysis [Hughes2005] using PetIGA [Dalcin2016]; linear algebra, time stepping, eigenproblems and MPI parallelism are provided by PETSc [PETSc] and SLEPc [Hernandez2005]. All quantities are in atomic units (a.u.).

Key features

Physics

  • Potentials \(V(\mathbf r)\) and effective masses \(m(\mathbf r)\) given as muParser expressions in the input file.

  • Hermitian BenDaniel–Duke kinetic operator \(-\tfrac12\nabla\cdot m^{-1}(\mathbf r)\nabla\) [BenDanielDuke1966].

  • Length-gauge coupling to laser fields along \(x\), \(y\) and \(z\); electrons (Charge = -1, default) or holes (Charge = +1).

  • Neumann or Dirichlet boundaries and a Manolopoulos complex absorbing potential (CAP) [Manolopoulos2002].

Discretisation and solvers

  • B-spline basis of degree p from 1 to 14 (Basis = bspline, default), or the \(C^0\) element bases bernstein, lagrange and spectral.

  • Uniform, graded, hydrogenic and interface-aligned knot sequences.

  • Bound states from shift-invert Krylov–Schur [Stewart2002] with an ordered list of inner linear solvers (direct LU, then iterative ASM/ILU), a retry with a larger subspace, and acceptance by the eigenpair backward error.

  • Time propagators cn (Crank–Nicolson [Crank1947]), pade22 (Padé(2,2) exponential midpoint rule) and magnus4 (fourth-order commutator-free Magnus [BlanesMoan2006] [Alvermann2011]).

  • Preconditioners asm (restricted additive Schwarz with ILU [CaiSarkis1999]), mg (geometric B-spline multigrid by knot insertion) and gamg (PETSc algebraic multigrid).

Observables and output

  • Dipole, velocity and acceleration, energy, currents, bound-state populations, autocorrelation and wavefunction snapshots, written to HDF5.

  • Photoelectron momentum spectra by t-SURFF [TaoScrinzi2012] (TSurff = 1).

  • Post-processors for transition dipole matrices and recombination dipoles.

Performance

  • Sum-factorised element assembly with BLAS contractions and banded insertion of element matrices.

  • Factorised (Kronecker) application of separable operators and quadrature-point evaluation of monitor expectation values in 2D and 3D.

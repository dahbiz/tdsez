HDF5 Output Schema

Complete dataset layout: dipole, energy, current, population, autocorrelation, eigenstates.

Ring Buffer

Rows are buffered in memory and appended to the file at most every 2 s of wall time (at multiples of OutputStrideTS), every 5000 steps, whenever the buffer is full, and at the end of the run. Every step is recorded; the buffering only affects when rows reach the file.

Time Evolution Data

File: td/TimeEvolutionData_*.h5

Dipole Table

Dataset dipoles, one row per step. With \(q\) = Charge:

  • 1D (4 columns): t, Ex, Dx, Ax

  • 2D (7 columns): t, Ex, Ey, Dx, Dy, Ax, Ay

  • 3D (10 columns): t, Ex, Ey, Ez, Dx, Dy, Dz, Ax, Ay, Az

where E* is the field at time t, \(D_a = q\langle x_a\rangle\) the dipole and \(A_a = q\, d\langle v_a\rangle/dt\) the dipole acceleration (\(= \ddot D_a\); see Observables). All spatial axes are recorded, including those not in Polarization (cross-polarized and asymmetric response); with ObserveAllAxes = 0 only the polarised axes are computed and the other columns are zero.

Energy Table

Col

Description

0

t – time

1

Kin – kinetic energy \(\langle \psi | K | \psi \rangle\)

2

Pot – potential energy \(\langle \psi | V | \psi \rangle\)

3

Int – interaction energy \(\mathbf E(t) \cdot \langle \mathbf r \rangle\), the expectation of the coupling term

4

Tot – total energy \(E_{\text{total}} = \text{Kin} + \text{Pot} + \text{Int}\)

5

InvMass – \(\langle \psi | 1/m(\mathbf{r}) | \psi \rangle\)

6

Norm – \(\langle \psi | M | \psi \rangle\) (norm conservation check)

Current Table

Dataset currents. Columns 0–2 are t, \(\langle L_z\rangle\) (2D/3D; 0 in 1D) and the Berry-phase increment \(d\Gamma\). The remaining columns are the current \(J_a = q\langle v_a\rangle\) (\(= \dot D_a\)) and its decomposition into intra-band, inter-band and bound–continuum parts:

  • 1D and 2D (11 columns): t, Lz, dGamma, Jx, Jy, Jx_intra, Jy_intra, Jx_inter, Jy_inter, Jx_bc, Jy_bc (Jy* are 0 in 1D)

  • 3D (15 columns): t, Lz, dGamma, Jx, Jy, Jz, Jx_intra, Jy_intra, Jz_intra, Jx_inter, Jy_inter, Jz_inter, Jx_bc, Jy_bc, Jz_bc

Population Table

Col

Description

0

t – time

1..N+1

\(P_n = |c_n|^2\) – population of bound state n

Autocorrelation

Col

Description

0

t

1

\(\text{Re} \langle \psi(0) | \psi(t) \rangle\)

2

\(\text{Im} \langle \psi(0) | \psi(t) \rangle\)

t-SURFF Spectra

File: td/surff_*.h5 (only with TSurff = 1). Datasets momenta, amplitude (Re, Im), pes \(=|b(\mathbf k)|^2\), on a grid also k_x/k_y/k_z and pes_grid; amplitude_flux with SurffInterior = 1. Attributes final_time, radius, exterior_mass, exterior_potential, charge, A_final, stride, n_surface_points, n_samples, interior. See Photoelectron Spectra (t-SURFF).

Eigenstate Data

File: static/EigenData_*.h5

Datasets: spectrum (1D, float64), psi_0, psi_1, … (complex vectors), run_metadata group with code_version, input_file, units, grid params, eig_residual (backward error \(\|Hv-EMv\|/((\|H\|_\infty+|E|\,\|M\|_\infty)\|v\|)\) of each kept state). Each axis dataset (knots_x, knots_y, knots_z) has attributes: mult_start_end (2-int), SplineDegree, nfuncs.

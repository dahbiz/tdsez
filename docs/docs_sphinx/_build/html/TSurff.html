Photoelectron Spectra (t-SURFF)

The time-dependent surface flux method (t-SURFF; Tao & Scrinzi, New J. Phys. 14, 013021 (2012)) computes momentum-resolved photoelectron spectra from the flux of the wave function through a surface that encloses the bound region, so the box only has to hold the bound dynamics and the quiver motion: the CAP behind the surface removes the outgoing electrons.

t-SURFF is a passive observer: it reads \(\psi\) at the sampled time steps and never changes the propagation. It is computed only when the input asks for it (TSurff = 1) and writes its own file, td/surff_<input>.h5.

Method

The surface is the box \(|x_d| = R\) (SurffRadius) on every axis (two points in 1D, the edges of a square in 2D, the faces of a cube in 3D). Outside it the carrier must be free: constant mass \(m\), constant potential \(V_0\) and the field coupling of the run,

\[H = -\frac{1}{2m}\nabla^2 + V_0 + s\,\mathbf E(t)\cdot\mathbf r , \qquad s = -q ,\ q = \text{Charge}.\]

Its length-gauge Volkov states are

\[\chi_{\mathbf k}(\mathbf r,t) = (2\pi)^{-d/2}\, e^{\,i(\mathbf k+\mathbf A(t))\cdot\mathbf r - i\Phi_{\mathbf k}(t)}, \qquad \dot{\mathbf A} = -s\,\mathbf E ,\quad \dot\Phi_{\mathbf k} = \frac{|\mathbf k+\mathbf A|^2}{2m} + V_0 ,\]

and the amplitude of the state that has left the box is the time integral of the flux through the surface,

\[b(\mathbf k) = \frac{i}{2}\int_0^T\!dt \oint dS\; \chi^*_{\mathbf k}\Big[-\frac{1}{m}\partial_n\psi - \frac{i}{m}\,\hat n\cdot(\mathbf k+\mathbf A)\,\psi\Big].\]

\(|b(\mathbf k)|^2 = dP/d^d k\) is the distribution of the canonical momentum \(\mathbf k\); the final kinetic momentum is \(\mathbf k + \mathbf A(T)\) (A_final in the file, zero for a pulse of zero area). The charge sign is the run’s (Charge = -1 electron, +1 hole), and the mass and potential are read from the Mass and Potential expressions at the surface.

Numerics:

  • \(\psi\) and \(m^{-1}\partial_n\psi\) at the surface quadrature points (Gauss points of every knot span inside \([-R, R]\), SurffQuadrature per span) come from one sparse product with a precomputed trace matrix; at set-up the trace is checked against direct B-spline evaluation.

  • The time integral uses the trapezoidal rule over the samples (every OutputStrideSurff-th step and the final time): second order in the sampling interval.

  • \(\mathbf A\), \(\int\mathbf A\) and \(\int(A^2/2m+V_0)\) are integrated with RK4 sub-steps of at most TimeStep/2, so the Volkov phase does not depend on the stride.

  • On a momentum grid the Volkov factors separate by axis: per sample and face the surface sum is one small GEMM plus axpy updates, instead of (#momenta × #surface points) complex exponentials. Each rank works on the surface points it owns; the amplitudes are reduced once at the end.

Before the run starts, the mass and potential are checked along outward rays from every surface point to the end of each axis; a deviation above SurffMassTolerance (relative) or SurffPotentialTolerance (Ha) stops the run. A Coulomb tail therefore has to be cut off smoothly before \(R\) (see the ATI example), or the tolerance raised knowingly.

InitialState = wavepacket starts from the Gaussian

\[\psi_0 = \prod_d (2\pi w_d^2)^{-1/4} \exp\!\Big(-\frac{(x_d-c_d)^2}{4w_d^2} + i k_{0,d}x_d\Big)\]

Choosing the parameters

  • Surface radius: outside the bound states and the potential (cut it off smoothly), and preferably outside the quiver radius \(\alpha = E_0/\omega^2\). The CAP starts behind it.

  • Final time: long enough after the pulse for the slowest electron of interest to reach the surface, \(T - T_{\rm pulse} \gtrsim (R-\text{core})/k_{\min}\); check by comparing two final times. For free-particle tests use SurffInterior = 1.

  • Momentum grid: \(k_{\max}\gtrsim\sqrt{2m\cdot 10U_p}\) for the full rescattering plateau; resolve the ATI spacing \(\Delta k \approx m\omega/k\).

  • Time step: t-SURFF inherits the propagator error; the rational propagators (pade22, magnus4) are much more accurate per step.

  • Charge: as for the run (-1 electron). Holes (+1) are free carriers with the opposite coupling sign; t-SURFF follows automatically.
